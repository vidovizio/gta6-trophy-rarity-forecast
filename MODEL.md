# Model and validation

The forecaster reconstructs earned counts as `rate × tracked players / 100`, so progress in trophy earning is not confused with growth or dilution of the tracked-player base. It forecasts the player base with a log-count trend over the latest 48 hours, capped at the recent 24-hour growth rate. For each trophy, it averages two median forecasts in logit space: a 48-hour logit-rate trend and a power-law earned-count trend against the tracked base, anchored at the latest non-null observation. Degenerate fits fall back to the remaining fit or the latest rate.

Logit uncertainty combines horizon-based random-walk spread, rate-trend residuals scaled by extrapolation leverage, and variation in consecutive refresh changes; it is bounded to 0.35–2.5. The five quantiles use the specified normal scores. Unambiguous story milestones are adjusted with non-increasing pool-adjacent-violators medians, after which quantiles are recalculated using each trophy's spread.

Validation used only the supplied practice cases and a generated synthetic case. Both practice outputs parsed with 55 valid rows (2,685 bytes each), satisfied quantile bounds/order, and were byte-identical across two runs. On flat p00 history, medians match the persistence starter exactly; mean 90% interval width was 1.0433 percentage points versus 0.3563 for the starter. In the two-day synthetic history, tracked players doubled from 100,000 to 200,000 at a refresh with a corresponding rate dip; chapter 1–4 forecast medians exceeded their last rates, while the 100%-completion and platinum medians remained below 1%. Its run took about 0.10 seconds. `python3 -m py_compile main.py` passed, and the entry imports only Python standard-library modules.

## Revision 2

The power-law earned-count slope is clamped to `[-1.0, 2.0]` before extrapolation, limiting the influence of noisy or sparse rate/count observations over the long target horizon. The forecasted tracked-player base is floored at its last observed value because the tracked population should not materially contract. These guards leave the other forecasting logic unchanged.

After revision, both practice cases still produced valid 55-row outputs of 2,685 bytes each, with ordered in-range quantiles and byte-identical results across repeated runs. Each output also exactly matched its pre-revision forecast on flat history. The regenerated two-day synthetic case produced a valid 55-row output (2,790 bytes): medians were 30.5818% for Chapter 1, 4.5178% for the final mission, 0.0443% for 100%-completion, 0.0100% for platinum, and 99.9900% for trophy 2. The chapter estimate rose above its last observed rate, while the completion milestones stayed low. Compilation with `python3 -m py_compile main.py` passed.
